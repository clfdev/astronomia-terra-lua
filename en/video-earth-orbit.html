This animation shows Earth in its elliptical orbit around the Sun, focusing on the seasons in Brazil.

Seasons are primarily caused by Earth's axial tilt (approximately 23.5°) relative to its orbital plane. This tilt causes different hemispheres to receive different amounts of sunlight throughout the year.

In Brazil (southern hemisphere), summer occurs when the south pole is tilted toward the Sun, receiving more direct sunlight. Winter occurs when the south pole is tilted away from the Sun.
