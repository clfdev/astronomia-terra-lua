Earth's Rotation and the Day/Night Cycle

The Earth rotates on its axis, completing a full rotation in approximately 24 hours. This rotation is responsible for the alternation between day and night that we experience.

As the Earth rotates, different parts of its surface face the Sun, receiving sunlight (day), while other parts face away from the Sun, experiencing darkness (night).

In this simulation, you can observe how Brazil moves through the day/night cycle as the Earth rotates. The highlighted area represents Brazil's approximate location on the globe.
